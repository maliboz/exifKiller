# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be useful, welcoming, and technically honest. Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent
- focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not people
- help newcomers learn without gatekeeping

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or discrimination
- hostile or insulting language
- doxxing, threats, or intimidation
- deliberately misleading security claims

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
